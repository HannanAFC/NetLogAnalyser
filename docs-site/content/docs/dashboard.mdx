Once logs are flowing, everything you need is in the sidebar of the app.

## Dashboard

The landing page after login. It shows headline numbers and sparklines for the selected time range, plus a
preview of your top anomalies so the most suspicious traffic is visible immediately.

## Live feed

Streams new entries as they are ingested, over a WebSocket. Each row shows the addresses, ports, protocol, size,
country and anomaly score.

## Log history

A scrollable table of everything stored, newest first. It uses cursor-based pagination, so it stays
fast and consistent even while new logs are arriving.

## Analytics

Charts for any time range up to the instance limit (30 days by default):

- **Timeseries**: traffic over time. The bucket size (e.g. per minute, per hour) is chosen automatically for the
  range, and you can override it. Very small buckets over a very long range are refused to keep responses small.
- **Top talkers**: the busiest sources or destinations, ranked by bytes or packets.
- **Protocol breakdown**: share of TCP, UDP, ICMP and other, with destination ports.

## Anomalies

Every entry with an anomaly score above a threshold (0.5 by default), highest first, with the **reasons** that
contributed to the score. See [Anomaly detection](/docs/anomaly-detection) for what each reason means.

## Geo map

A world choropleth of traffic by country, with a sidebar for the selected country.

## Settings

- **Account**: change display name and password, logout or delete your account and all data.
- **API keys**: create and revoke keys, see when each was last used.
- **Exports**: request a download of your data, see [Exports and retention](/docs/exports-and-retention).

## Time ranges

Pages that show aggregates share a time range picker. Ranges are limited by `ANALYTICS_MAX_RANGE_DAYS`
(30 by default). Summary and timeseries responses are cached briefly (30 seconds by default), so a brand-new
entry can take up to that long to appear, though it shows in the live feed instantly.
