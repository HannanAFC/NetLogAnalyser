## Exporting your data

From **Settings → Exports** you can request a copy of all your logs.

1. Click **Request export**. The request is queued and returns immediately.
2. A background worker streams your logs, oldest first, into `logs.csv` and `logs.jsonl`, zips them and stores
   the file.
3. When the status is complete, click **Download**.

Exports can be large, which is why they run in the background rather than during the request. If the worker is
restarted mid-job, the job is retried automatically (up to 3 attempts).

### Download links and limits

- Download links are short-lived (5 minutes by default) and require you to be logged in. Files are never served
  from a permanent public URL.
- Export files expire after 72 hours by default and are then deleted.
- Each user can request a limited number of manual exports per day (3 by default).

## Data retention

To keep storage bounded, logs older than the retention period (30 days by default) are deleted. This happens in
two phases so nobody loses data by surprise:

1. **Warn.** For every user with logs about to expire, the system generates an export and emails them a download
   link, giving a grace period (7 days by default).
2. **Delete.** After the grace period, expired logs are deleted, but **only for users who have a confirmed
   warning export**. If a user's export or email failed, their data is skipped, never silently deleted.

Deletion happens in chunks per user, so it never holds a long database transaction.

## Notes for self-hosters

When self-hosting, it may be worth adjusting export and retention settings, you can adjust where the exports are
stored e.g. S3 vs local and export and link lifetimes. You can also adjust retention periods or disable retention
entirely.

<Callout>
  The retention jobs run on a schedule outside the API process. Self-hosters need to schedule them, see
  [Operating your instance](/docs/selfhost-operations#scheduling-retention).
</Callout>

## Deleting your account

**Settings → Account → Delete account** removes your user, API keys and all log entries. Deletion runs in the
background and cannot be undone.
